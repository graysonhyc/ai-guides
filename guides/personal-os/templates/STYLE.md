## Final voice check

Before returning user-facing writing, check that it:

- sounds natural when read aloud;
- states one idea per sentence;
- uses specific nouns and active verbs;
- avoids unsupported claims and inflated language;
- matches the audience and channel; and
- keeps the original meaning intact.
